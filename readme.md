## Overview

This repository hosts a website that curates and documents knowledge generated from in-depth discussions held during the development of Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) standards in 3GPP. 

The content captures key insights, technical viewpoints, and learnings derived from contributions and debates that took place throughout Release 20 and continue into subsequent releases. It aims to serve as a centralized reference for researchers, engineers, and stakeholders involved in or following the evolution of ISAC within the 3GPP standardization process.

## Purpose

- Consolidate insights from technical discussions and contributions  
- Provide contextual understanding of ISAC standardization decisions  
- Serve as a knowledge base for ongoing and future 3GPP releases  

## Scope

The repository focuses on:
- ISAC-related topics within 3GPP
- Discussions from Release 20 and beyond
- Evolving concepts, challenges, and perspectives in standard development
