Research Publications: SLS Team

  1. Rahul Pal, Vikram Singh, and Vijay Mareedu, “Performance Evaluation of AI-based CSI Feedback Techniques for 5G Advance and 6G Networks” manuscript ID: PHYCOM-D-24-01425R2 has been accepted on Aug-03/2025 for publication in Physical Communication Journal..

  2. Adarsh Ravi, Divyansh Gupta, Vikram Singh, and Preetam Kumar, “SuperConvNet: Super Resolution Orchestrated Deep Convolutional Neural Network for Spatial Beam Prediction in B5G and 6G Systems” has been submitted to IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications.

  3. Aditya Gupta, Vikram Singh, and Preetam Kumar, “PosNet: A CNN-based Transformer model for UE localization in NLOS dominated scenarios” has been submitted to IEEE Communications Letters.
