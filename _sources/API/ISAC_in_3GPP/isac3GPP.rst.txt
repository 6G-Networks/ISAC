==============================================
ISAC in 3GPP
==============================================

The following table provides the URL of the 3GPP documents related to ISAC and some useful 
references for each ISAC feature. The table will be updated as and when new documents are 
released by 3GPP and new references are added. The link are attached from the latest version
of the documents. Please check the version of the document before referring to it.

5G-New Radio Tracks
---------------------

The details of work carried out in 3GPP related to ISAC in 5G-New Radio are as follows:

- Stage-1/SA1 (Service Requirements): `TR 22.837 Release-19  Study on Integrated Sensing and Communication <https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/22_series/22.837/22837-j40.zip>`_
- Stage-1/SA1: `3GPP TS 22.137 Release-19 Integrated Sensing and Communication <https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/22_series/22.137/22137-j10.zip>`_.
- ISAC Channel Model: `TR 3GPP 38.901 (version later than 19.3.0) <https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/38_series/38.901/>`_.
- RAN1/RAN2/RAN3: `3GPP TR 38.765: Study on Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) for NR Release-20 <https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/38_series/38.765/38765-100.zip>`_.


ISAC Work in 5G-NR: Release-19
*********************************

The work related to ISAC in 5G-New Radio started in Release-19 with focus on primarily two aspects: 
1. ISAC usecases and requirements in **SA-WG1**, and 
2. ISAC channel modeling in **RAN-WG1**.

ISAC Work in 5G-NR: Release-20
*********************************

The work related to ISAC in 5G-New Radio started in Release-19 with focus on primarily two aspects: 
1. ISAC for gNB based Monostatic Sensing in **RAN-WG1**, 
2. Radio Access Network Architecture for ISAC in **RAN-WG3**, and
3. System Architecture for ISAC in **SA-WG2**.
