==============================================
3GPP Standardization Work
==============================================

SLS team assists the 3GPP standards team with standardization activities which is limited to:

- Filing and Protecting IPRs/Patents,
- Assist all the member of 3GPP standards team with the simulations and evaluations,
- Submission of TDocs for pushing IPRs to 3GPP standards,
- Observe and participate in 3GPP Meetings.

Assistance for 3GPP Standardization
####################################

Following are the contributions and the IPs filed to
protect the ideas pushed via the contributions. SLS teams are working on following topics:

- **(9.1) AI/ML for NR Air Interface**:
    - (9.1.2) For Positioning Enhancements, (**Vikram**)
    - (9.1.4) Other aspects of AI/ML model and data. (**Vijay**)
- **(9.7) Study on channel modelling for ISAC for NR**
    - (9.7.1) ISAC deployment scenarios (**Vikram**)
    - (9.7.2) ISAC channel modelling (**Raghava/Salija, Vijay, Vikram**)

Team is responsible for:

- submitting contribution to protect IPRs,
- read the contributions submitted by the other companies,
- prepare the set of comments to critique/align with other companies and defend our submissions,
- follow all the online meetings diligently,
- provide comments to all round of discussion over the 5 days,
- in case of criticality take help on floor from the Tejas delegate,
- if Tejas delegates are constrained, borrow help from CEWiT/IITM/IITK delegate (iff necessary)
